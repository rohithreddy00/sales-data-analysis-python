
Sales Data Analysis Using Python and SQL

 Project Overview
This Data Analytics project analyzes sample sales data using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and SQL to understand sales performance, product revenue, customer behavior, and monthly sales trends.

 Objectives
- Calculate total sales revenue and quantity sold.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Examine customer purchasing behavior.
- Summarize key sales performance indicators (KPIs).
- Visualize sales insights using charts.

- **Matplotlib** — Data visualization
- **SQL** — Sales analysis and reporting
- **Google Colab** — Notebook environment

Key Business Insights

Based on the sales data analysis, the following business insights can be derived:

- **Product Performance:** Compare revenue generated by each product to identify high-revenue products and understand their contribution to overall sales.

- **Sales Volume:** Analyze the quantity sold for each product to identify popular products and support inventory planning.

- **Monthly Sales Trends:** Examine monthly revenue patterns to identify stronger and weaker sales periods and support sales forecasting.

- **Revenue Contribution:** Evaluate each product's percentage share of total revenue to understand which products contribute most to business performance.

- **Monthly Sales Volume:** Compare the number of units sold across months to identify changes in customer demand and plan inventory accordingly.

Business Recommendations

- Prioritize inventory planning based on product demand and sales performance.
- Develop marketing strategies to improve sales of lower-performing products.
- Use monthly sales trends to support demand forecasting and resource allocation.
- Monitor revenue contribution by product to support pricing and product portfolio decisions.
- Review sales KPIs regularly to track performance and identify improvement opportunities.

 Python Analysis
1. Open [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/).
2. Download `Sales_Data_Analysis.ipynb` from this repository.
3. Upload the notebook to Google Colab.
4. Run the cells from top to bottom.

 SQL Analysis
1. Open the SQL files in the `sql/` folder.
2. Review each query.
3. Run the queries in a compatible SQL environment after configuring the dataset and table structure required by the queries.
